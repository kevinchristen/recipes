# Sauted Mushrooms

From [Epicurious](https://www.epicurious.com/recipes/food/views/sauteed-mushrooms-102741). Also inspired by a dish we had at Lavomatique in Nice. 30 minutes.

## Ingredients

* 1 T fresh lemon juice
* 1/2 T soy sauce
* 2 T Marsala wine
* 2 T olive oil
* 10 ounces mushrooms. At Lavomatique they used /Champignons de Paris/, which are just button mushrooms.
* 1 T unsalted butter
* 1 garlic clove, chopped

## Directions

1. Trim mushroom stems, then half or quarter mushrooms.
2. Heat a 12-inch iron skillet over moderately high heat until very hot. Add oil, then sauté mushrooms, stirring, until golden brown, about 5 minutes.
3. Add butter and garlic and saute until butter is absorbed.
4. Add lemon mixture to mushrooms, stirring until sauce is absorbed.
