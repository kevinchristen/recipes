# Frijoles Charros (Cowboy Beans)

From [Serious Eats](https://www.seriouseats.com/frijoles-charros-mexican-pinto-beans-bacon-recipe). Overnight soaking plus 4 hours.

## Ingredients

* 1 pound (450g) dried pinto beans
* Kosher salt
* 6 cups (1.4L) homemade or store-bought low-sodium chicken stock
* 2 bay leaves
* 2 sprigs epazote (optional)
* 12 ounces (340g) diced bacon
* 1 medium white or yellow onion, diced
* 2 serrano chiles, minced, seeds removed
* 1 jalapeño, minced, seeds removed
* 3 medium cloves garlic, minced
* 2 14-ounce cans diced fire-roasted tomatoes
* Large handful chopped fresh cilantro leaves and fine stems

## Instructions

1. Soak beans overnight covered in water with 2 T kosher salt, then drain and rinse.
2. Add beans, stock, bay leaves, epazote, and 2 t kosher salt to a dutch oven. Bring to a boil, then simmer, covered, until beans soften (about 45 minutes).
3. Render bacon in a large skillet, and continue to cook until just starting to brown.
4. Add onions and chiles, and continue to saute until onions soften.
5. Add minced garlic and stir for 30 seconds, then add tomatos. Saute until liquid thickens.
6. Add to skillet contents to beans and continue to cook covered until done, 1.5-3 hours.
7. Discard bay leaves, add cilantro, season to taste, and serve.

## Nutrition (one serving of eight)

* 281 kC
* 28g Carbs
* 12g Fat
* 15g Protein
