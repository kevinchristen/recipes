# Mashed Potatoes

From [Barefoot Contessa](https://barefootcontessa.com/recipes/buttermilk-mashed-potatoes). 1 hr.

## Ingredients

* Kosher salt
* 3 pounds Yukon Gold potatoes
* 1/2 cup whole milk
* 1/4 pound (1 stick) unsalted butter
* 3/4 to 1 cup buttermilk, shaken
* 1/2 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper

## Instructions

1. Peel potatoes and cut into 1.5 inch chunks.
2. Simmer until completely tender in water and 2 T salt, about 15 minutes.
3. In a saucepan, warm milk and butter. Don't boil.
4. Drain and mash potatoes, then fold in milk, butter, 2 t salt, and pepper.
5. Fold in buttermilk.
