# Carbonara

From [Serious Eats](https://www.seriouseats.com/pasta-carbonara-sauce-recipe). 30 minutes.

## Ingredients (1 or 2 servings)

* 110/220 gm pasta. Spaghetti, Bucatini, or Penne will all work.
* 1/2 T olive oil
* 20/40 gm guanciale. Bacon is an adequate substitute.
* 1/1 whole egg
* 1/2 egg yolks
* 8/16 gm pecorino
* 8/16 gm parmesan
* pepper
* kosher salt

## Directions

1. Heat salted water for pasta.
2. Finely dice the guanciale. Cook it in the olive oil until fat is rendered and the rest is crispy. When ready, lower the pan's temperature to warm.
3. In a bowl, mix the eggs and grated cheese.
4. To the bowl, add pepper and about a 3-finger pinch of kosher salt.
5. When the pasta is al dente, drag it with some of its water into the guanciale pan. Add the egg and cheese mixture, and "mop" the pasta through it all to create an emulsion. The dish will quickly thicken as it cools on a plate, so add more pasta water to loosen before serving.
