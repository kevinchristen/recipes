# Pizza Dough

From [So Much Food Blog Same Day Pizza Dough](https://somuchfoodblog.com/same-day-pizza-dough/). 5 hours.

## Ingredients

* Bread flour
* Dry yeast
* Salt
* Water
* Olive oil

### Quantities

| # of pies | Bread flour (gm) | Yeast (teaspoon) | Salt (gm) | Water (gm) | Olive Oil (Tablespoon) |
| --------- | ---------------- | ---------------- | --------- | ---------- | ---------------------- |
| 3 | 500 | 1 | 10 | 290 | 2 |
| 4 | 600 | 1 | 12 | 350 | 2 |

## Instructions

1. Mix dry ingredients in mixer bowl
2. Add oil, then water (gradually) as mixer runs on slow with dough hook. Add water until a dough ball forms and cleans the side of the bowl. Kneed for 5-10 minutes.
3. Form into a ball on a work surface dusted with flour, then put in a oiled bowl, cover with plastic wrap, and allow to rise for about 2 hours or until doubled.
4. Punch down dough, divide into balls for each pizza, kneed and reform into balls.
5. Place on an oiled baking sheet for proofing.
6. Proof for about another 2 hours.
