# Hasselback Potatoes

From [Recipe Tin Eats](https://www.recipetineats.com/hasselback-potatoes/). 1 hour 30 minutes.

## Ingredients

* Potatoes
* Olive oil
* Kosher salt
* Garlic powder (optional)

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 400 ℉.
2. Flatten one side of potatoes by slicing off the rounded part.
3. Place each potato on its flat side between two chopsticks, and slice across until the knife hits the chopsticks. Make parallel cuts about 2 mm apart. Leave some unsliced at the ends, or you will slice the ends off.
4. Brush with oil, sprinkle with salt, and bake for 30 min on a baking sheet.
5. The potatoes should be beginning to fan out. Brush with more oil, then salt and apply garlic powder if desired.
6. Return to oven and bake for 40 minutes, basting with olive oil from the pan every 10 minutes.
7. Finish at 425 ℉ for 10 min.
